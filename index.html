01. Professional Dossier

I approach cybersecurity with an investigator's mindset developed over five years of diagnosing complex, high-stakes infrastructure failures in the mechanical and drain cleaning industry. In mechanical systems, catastrophic failures leave physical evidence—pressure drops, flow restrictions, thermal cracking. In digital systems, the physics change, but the forensic principles remain identical: follow the telemetry, establish the timeline, isolate the root cause, and verify containment.

Currently completing rigorous foundations in the NIST Risk Management Framework, SIEM log analysis (Splunk, Google Chronicle), defensive Python scripting, and incident response playbooks.
