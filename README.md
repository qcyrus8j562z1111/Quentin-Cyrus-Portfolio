## 📌 Executive Summary

Dedicated cybersecurity specialist transitioning **5+ years of mechanical diagnostics and critical infrastructure troubleshooting** into **Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR)**. 

In mechanical diagnostic work, catastrophic failures leave physical telemetry—pressure drops, flow restrictions, thermal cracking, and material stress. In digital systems, the telemetry shifts to packets, logs, and memory states, but the forensic principles remain identical:
1. **Isolate the Anomaly:** Detect irregularities against established baseline behavior.
2. **Preserve Evidence Integrity:** Maintain rigorous chain of custody and deterministic logging.
3. **Determine Root Cause:** Move past surface symptoms to pinpoint the exact failure vector or threat actor.
4. **Remediate & Harden:** Implement proactive controls to prevent recurring compromise.
